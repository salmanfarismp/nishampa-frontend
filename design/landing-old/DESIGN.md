## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a bridge between authoritative statesmanship and intimate human warmth. The aesthetic strategy pairs an immersive, dramatic royal navy header and hero zone with an illuminated, breathable ivory editorial sanctuary below. It avoids sterile corporate minimalism in favor of structured editorial warmth—evoking the prestige of heritage institutions, long-form journals, and thoughtful private-office craftsmanship.

Key brand tenets:
- **Dignified yet Approachable:** Authoritative structure paired with generous breathing room, avoiding aloofness or institutional coldness.
- **Luminous Tension:** Strategic dynamic between light and shadow—the deep oceanic density of the header juxtaposed against warm, parchment-tinted field surfaces.
- **Grounded Tactility:** Restrained, precise borders, warm ember micro-accents, and fine typographic scale over loud dropshadows.

## Colors

The palette establishes a dual-atmosphere environment. The hero environment operates in a concentrated night mode, while the primary interactive surface rests upon an ivory-warmed light plane.

- **Primary (`#023A72`):** Deep Royal Navy. Anchors top-level chrome, navigation, hero headers, and crucial focal actions. In lighter environments, used for primary typography and high-emphasis boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#E2B023`):** Luminous Warm Gold. Serves as focal inflection points: active indicator states, accent crests, subtle badge highlights, and high-priority glyphs. Never used over large background masses; applied strictly as an illuminant.
- **Tertiary (`#B8860B`):** Deep Burnished Ochre. Provides accessible text contrast for secondary accents on light surfaces, borders, and tactile hover states.
- **Neutral Core (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm Ivory. Replaces stark optical white across main page containers, softening reading contrast and providing tactile, paper-like grounding.
- **Supporting Neutral Dark (`#0E1A29`):** Midnight Slate. The deepest anchor shade for text headings in the light domain and rich shadows.
- **Supporting Neutral Soft (`#EFECE4`):** Raw Linen. Used for subtle hairline dividers and muted card containers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally contrasts classic optical serif qualities with high-legibility neo-grotesque sans-serif forms.

- **Headlines & Display (Newsreader):** Brings editorial prestige, subtle calligraphic terminal hooks, and literary gravitas. Display text must use italic variants sparingly for emphasis, pulled quotes, and key descriptive adjectives.
- **Interface & Content Structure (Inter):** Handles high-density data, metadata labels, inputs, and lengthy interface texts. Ensures maximum legibility across screen densities.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with slightly extended tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in bold or medium Inter weights to maintain architectural poise across small footprints.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive layout engine based on a dynamic 8pt rhythmic base.

### Viewport Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutter: `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Canvas margin: `1rem` (`margin-sm`). Elements stack strictly vertical. Hero sections feature condensed height to present immediate body content hooks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Gutter: `1.5rem` (`gutter`). Canvas margin: `2rem`. Two-column symmetrical or 5-3 asymmetrical divisions.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max structural container. Gutter: `2rem` (`gutter-lg`). Canvas margin: `4rem` (`margin-lg`). Accommodates offset editorial margins, wide breathing expanses, and floating summary sidebars.

### Spatial Rhythm
Outer boundaries and structural section blocks use `space-xl` and higher for clear sectional cadence. Internal element composition (e.g., card padding, form stacks) strictly relies on `space-sm` through `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through fine tonal boundaries and gentle surface layering rather than intense drop shadows.

- **Hero & Navy Surface Depth:** Within deep navy spaces (`#023A72`), depth relies on subtle atmospheric gradients and hairline borders tinted with 12% gold opacity (`rgba(226, 176, 35, 0.12)`). Elevated floating cards in the dark canvas receive an inner luminous glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(226, 176, 35, 0.2), 0 12px 32px rgba(2, 26, 52, 0.4)`.
- **Light Editorial Canvas Depth:** On ivory surfaces (`#FBF9F5`), standard cards and modals use low-contrast, warm-tinted outlines (`border: 1px solid #EFECE4`) paired with ambient diffused shading: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(14, 26, 41, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(14, 26, 41, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Floating overlays introduce soft elevation: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(2, 58, 114, 0.12), 0 1px 3px rgba(14, 26, 41, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Corner radii are deliberately subdued to preserve an architectural, editorial posture.

- **Standard Base Radii:** Form fields, buttons, and micro-containers feature a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- **Cards & Surface Modules (`rounded-lg`):** Use `0.5rem` (8px) to soften the perimeter while sustaining typographic alignment.
- **Large Modals & Feature Canvas Panels (`rounded-xl`):** Capped at `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted solely to micro-tags, notification pills, and status tags (`border-radius: 9999px`) where immediate visual differentiation is needed.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#023A72`) background, crisp ivory text (`#FBF9F5`), `0.25rem` radius, subtle 1px border (`#012B55`). Focus ring: 2px offset with luminous gold (`#E2B023`).
- **Accent Hero Action:** Luminous Warm Gold (`#E2B023`) background, dark navy text (`#02264C`), medium weight Inter. Transitions on hover with a 2% brightness increase and delicate drop glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px solid boundary in `#023A72` (or `rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.3)` on dark navy hero), with font matching boundary color.
- **Ghost:** Zero border, padding-aligned, Navy text with background fading to `rgba(2, 58, 114, 0.04)` on hover.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Constructed on a warm base (`#FFFFFF` on `#FBF9F5` canvas).
- Border: 1px solid `#DDD9CE`.
- Active/Focus State: Border snaps to `#023A72` with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 58, 114, 0.1)`.
- Labeling: `label-md` uppercase tracked metadata sitting neatly above the control.

### Cards & Editorial Containers
- Base light background `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px solid `#EFECE4` outline.
- Inside padding set to `space-lg` (1.5rem).
- Optional decorative variant: An accent top hairline border (2px) in Warm Gold (`#E2B023`) to denote featured status.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Chip:** Warm Ivory fill (`#F3EFE6`), `#023A72` text, 1px border in `#E5E0D3`. Rounded-full.
- **Gold Accent Indicator:** Tinted amber background (`rgba(226, 176, 35, 0.12)`), solid `#996E00` text, highlighting critical status or curated content.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Crisp square (checkbox, 2px radius) and circle (radio).
- Default: 1.5px stroke `#B4AEA0`, ivory fill.
- Checked: Fill `#023A72`, inner checkmark/pip rendered in luminous gold (`#E2B023`).

### Editorial Blockquotes & Callouts
- Left-bordered inset (3px solid `#E2B023`).
- Typography set in `Newsreader` italic, 1.25rem, with primary navy coloration (`#023A72`).
- Integrated attribution set in Inter bold `label-sm` tracking.
